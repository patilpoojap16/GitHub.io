    Pharmaceutical Document Intelligence Pipeline Pfizer externship, completed through Extern in partnership with Pfizer

    Built an end-to-end pipeline that reads pharmaceutical vendor files with OCR, retrieves answers with a LlamaIndex RAG framework, and routes documents by type. Benchmarked three OCR engines and compared chunking strategies, dense and sparse retrieval, and embedding models (MiniLM, E5, BGE). Then compared Mistral-7B, TinyLlama, and Phi-2 on hallucination rate and context use to write a deployment recommendation, delivered with a technical report and an interactive demo.

    Juvenile Justice Predictive Analytics Pipeline PwC externship, completed through Extern in partnership with PwC

    Processed more than 25 years of longitudinal data across five datasets (facility climate, incident reports, administrative records, staff surveys, and outcome measures) to find predictors of youth safety outcomes such as youth and staff fear rates and recidivism. Matched every research question to a fitting statistical method, documented each decision on missing data and variable selection, and interpreted effect sizes against policy-relevant thresholds. Findings went to nonprofit leaders and state agency stakeholders as briefs and presentations.

    Undiagnosed Glycemic Conditions in U.S. Adults Columbia group project using NHANES data

    Fit nested logistic regression models in SAS on NHANES survey data and chose the classification threshold with Youden's J index. Tested associations stratified by age, BMI, and race and ethnicity using Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel methods. Sensitivity analyses applied survey weights and the complex sampling design to produce population-representative estimates of undiagnosed diabetes and prediabetes.

    • SAS
    • Logistic regression
    • Youden's J
    • Survey weighting

    Implemented the HN-Adam adaptive optimization algorithm from scratch in PyTorch. Benchmarked convergence speed, loss trajectory, and final test accuracy against standard Adam and SGD across neural network training tasks, and analyzed sensitivity to learning rate schedules and batch size.
